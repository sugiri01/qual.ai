Implementation Strategy

Key Focus Areas

  • Direct Course Leads (42% profiles): Major focus with 721 profiles dedicated to immediate course conversions across AI/ML, Python, SQL, and Data Analytics
  • BDM (17% profiles): 300 profiles of experienced professionals with education sector background for business development
  • Trainers (15% profiles): Technical experts with specialized teaching experience in our core offerings
  • Telecallers & Admin-HR (13% each): Balanced allocation to support operations and outreach efforts
  • Technical - Full Stack: Dedicated hot job and email campaign for specialized technical requirements

Implementation Benefits

  • Strong focus on direct revenue generation with 42% of profiles allocated to course leads
  • Balanced support roles to maintain operational efficiency
  • Strategic email distribution focusing on both course leads and recruitment
  • Hot job postings targeting key technical and training positions
  • Dedicated technical recruitment channel for Full Stack developers
  • Higher conversion potential with increased focus on direct course leads

Lead Conversion Strategy

With 721 profiles (42% of total) allocated specifically as direct course leads, we have a significant opportunity for immediate revenue generation:

  • AI & ML: 216 profiles (30% of leads) with technical backgrounds ready for advanced training
  • Python Programming: 187 profiles (26% of leads) seeking programming skills for career advancement
  • SQL & Database: 173 profiles (24% of leads) needing data management capabilities
  • Data Analytics: 145 profiles (20% of leads) from various sectors looking to develop analytical skills

With this substantial lead pool, we should implement an aggressive 30-day conversion campaign targeting minimum 20% immediate conversion rate (144+ enrollments).

Phase 1: Initial Contact (Days 1-7)

  • Segment all 721 leads into 4 course categories
  • Targeted email campaign with course-specific benefits
  • Follow-up calls to high-potential leads (top 30%)
  • Free webinar invitations with course previews

Phase 2: Engagement (Days 8-20)

  • Targeted follow-up calls to all 721 profiles
  • Personalized course demos for interested leads
  • Industry-specific success stories and testimonials
  • Time-limited early enrollment discounts

Phase 3: Conversion (Days 21-30)

  • Final offer with special incentives for immediate enrollment
  • Flexible payment options and installment plans
  • Group discount offers for company/team enrollments
  • Job placement assistance for course completers
